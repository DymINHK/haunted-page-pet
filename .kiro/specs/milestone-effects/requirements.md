# Requirements — Milestone Effects

## R1 Milestone celebrations
As a visitor, I want big haunted celebrations when the community counter hits iconic
numbers, so that clicking feels like a shared event.
- WHEN the total count crosses 520, 888, 1314 or 9999 THE SYSTEM SHALL play a
  full-screen Canvas celebration themed to the number.
- WHEN the total count crosses **666** THE SYSTEM SHALL play the headline effect:
  rolling fog, moonlight flash and a pumpkin rain.
- WHEN an effect finishes THE SYSTEM SHALL remove its Canvas and release its
  resources, leaving no DOM or memory residue.
- WHERE the visitor prefers reduced motion THE SYSTEM SHALL shorten effects and
  reduce particle counts.
- Effects SHALL NOT block interaction for more than 3 seconds.
